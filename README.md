# Richies-Handy-Tools
Calculator for ease of making correct ratio hummingbird food from raw, granulated sugar, syrup made from sugar. This allows you to decide if you want to make a certain amount of finish feed and work backwords by selecting the finished food at top, or you can batch with just a measure of raw granulated sugar to either make a final batch or a syrup to use later for ease of storage. Since syrup stores more compact, you can save space by storing it in that form. However, syrup won't formulate the same way in a dilution because sugar dissolves into a much smaller space than granular form which makes making a finished food from syrup more challenging. This calculator lets you add the quantity of syrup and how much water to add to make the proper dillution. It also allows you to enter the finished batch size and it tells you the volume of syrup to use. Use this feature by choosing the 'make food from leftover syrup' option
